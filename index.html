About

Who I Am

I'm a Supply Chain Management graduate who has spent the last few years learning how operations actually break, and how to keep them running anyway. At Enterprise Mobility, that meant managing a 250-plus vehicle rental branch through sales, CRM, procurement, and vendor negotiations. At Lockheed Martin, it means tracking parts, coordinating suppliers, and making sure nothing slows down a jet.

I was accepted into the University of Arkansas's M.S. Product Innovation program, and I'm using it to build on the analytical, process-driven habits I picked up in the field — the kind you only really learn when the systems are under pressure.

Field note

A hailstorm damaged over 80% of the vehicles at my Enterprise branch. Working through the fleet recovery — inspections, vendor coordination, customer communication — is where I learned what real problem-solving under pressure looks like.
